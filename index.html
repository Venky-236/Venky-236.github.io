02 — Innovations

Idea → Execution

Every project below started as my own idea. Each card shows where the idea came from and what I actually built.

Featured Jan – Aug 2026 · Independent chip design

IVCU-EV V4 Dual-mode EV safety SoC

Idea
After a crash, an electric vehicle's battery pack can still be live. I designed a controller around one requirement: cut high voltage within 50 ms of a crash, in a car or a motorcycle, from the same chip.
Execution
About two months re-architecting the RTL until it synthesized clean, then the full physical-design flow on SkyWater 130 nm: floorplan, power grid, placement, clock tree and routing — to a DRC-clean routed layout.
  • Verilog-2001
  • Yosys + ABC
  • OpenSTA
  • OpenRAM
  • OpenROAD
  • Sky130

Final-year project · Published 2025

Farmer Safety System AI snake detection & bite alert

Idea
It came from a personal loss to a snakebite. Farmers work far from help, so the field itself should warn them — and raise an alarm if someone is bitten.
Execution
A two-person build: motion, thermal and sound sensors with a TensorFlow Lite model on a Raspberry Pi 4, an Arduino Nano body-temperature check for bites, solar powered.
  • 1st place — Engineers' Day Project Expo, 2023
  • 1st prize — College Hackathon, 2024
  • Raspberry Pi 4
  • TensorFlow Lite
  • OpenCV
  • Arduino

Jan – Feb 2025

Smart Railway Junction Automatic track switching

Idea
Manual signalling is a single point of failure. A junction should switch itself — and refuse to send a train into an occupied loop.
Execution
Three ultrasonic sensors and a servo on an Arduino. The junction switches in under 0.5 s, with a safety lock that brakes instead of switching into a collision. Tested over 50+ cycles.
  • 1st prize — Electrical Project Expo, SUNRISE 2K25 (national-level fest)
  • Arduino Uno
  • HC-SR04
  • Servo control
  • State machine

Oct – Dec 2025 · In progress

Guardian Band Women's safety wearable SoC

Idea
A safety device that looks like an ordinary bangle: no phone, no app, no tracking. Five taps by the wearer — or signs that she is unconscious — send an alert with her location.
Execution
RTL for the tap detector, motion monitor, power manager, GPS and cellular blocks, taken through Synopsys Design Compiler and IC Compiler II on a 14 nm teaching library, with clock gating for low power.
  • Verilog
  • Design Compiler
  • IC Compiler II
  • UPF

03 — Research & training

Published, and trained on industry tools.

Published IJSRED · Mar – Apr 2025

Farmer's Safety from Reptiles

International Journal of Scientific Research and Engineering Development, Vol. 8, Issue 2, pp. 848–854 · ISSN 2581-7175 · Open access

An IoT system that detects snakes in the field — motion, thermal and sound sensors confirmed by a TensorFlow Lite model on a Raspberry Pi 4 — raises local and mobile alerts, and watches for signs of a bite. Solar powered, for fields with no mains supply.

My part: the idea was mine. Two of us built it end to end — hardware, sensor integration, the ML model and testing. Co-author, 5 of 6.
